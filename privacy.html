Privacy Policy

DoneStar is a personal desktop app. It stores its data only on the owner's computer.

If the owner connects a Google account, DoneStar uses Google Calendar and Google Tasks permissions only to read and write one calendar named DoneStar and one task list. It also reads the account's email address to show which account is connected.

Google data is not sent to the developer or to any third party. DoneStar has no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. The owner can disconnect at any time in the app or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
